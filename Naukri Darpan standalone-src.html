Home/About

How we verify listings

What goes into a listing before it appears on Naukri Darpan.

Our process

Every listing starts from a government board's own notification — the PDF released by SSC, UPSC, IBPS, a Railway Recruitment Board or a state body. We read the source document, extract the dates, fee, eligibility and links, and publish a plain-language summary here.

What we don't do

We don't accept payment to list, prioritise or remove a post. We don't charge candidates for information that's freely published by the government. If a listing looks wrong, use the "Report an issue" link on that page and we'll check it against the source.

This demo build

This particular build of the site uses placeholder data for demonstration — dates, fees and vacancy numbers are illustrative rather than pulled from live notifications, except for the syllabus pages, which are researched from public exam-preparation sources.
